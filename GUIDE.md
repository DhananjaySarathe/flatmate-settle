# ExpenseWaale - User Guide

ExpenseWaale keeps track of shared costs for a flat, a trip, or any group, and tells you who pays whom to settle up, using as few payments as possible.

## Getting Started

1. **Create an account** on the sign-in page with your name, email and a password of at least 6 characters. If your project requires email confirmation, check your inbox and open the link, then sign in.
2. **A "Default" space is ready** as soon as you sign up. Home asks who you're splitting with. Add everyone's names, including your own.
3. **Log your first expense** with the **Add expense** button.

Only you need an account. The other people in a space are just names, and you share results with them through a link, a PDF, or a copied summary.

## Finding Your Way Around

| Section | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Home** | Where you stand, the payments that settle everyone up, and recent expenses |
| **Expenses** | Every expense in the space, grouped by month, with search and filters |
| **Balances** | Balances and the settle-up plan for any period, with filters, exports and settled periods |
| **Insights** | Spending trends, cost per person, and the leaderboard |
| **People** | Add, edit or remove the people in the current space |
| **Spaces** | Create, switch, rename or delete spaces |

- **On a computer**, everything is in the left sidebar. The space switcher sits at the top and the **Add expense** button just below it.
- **On a phone**, the tab bar at the bottom has Home, Expenses, a big **+** for adding an expense, Balances and **More** (Insights, People, Spaces, sign out). Switch spaces from the name at the top of the screen.
- **Keyboard shortcut**: press **N** anywhere to add an expense.

## Spaces

A space keeps its own people, expenses and settled periods, so your flat and a weekend trip never mix.

- **Switch spaces** with the switcher (sidebar on a computer, top of the screen on a phone).
- **Create a space** from the switcher's **New space** item or on the **Spaces** page.
- **Rename** a space with the pencil icon on the Spaces page.
- **Delete** a space with the trash icon. You can only delete a space that's empty: no people and no expenses. Your **Default** space can't be deleted.

## People

People are whoever shares costs in a space: flatmates, friends, colleagues. They don't need accounts and don't get invites.

### Adding someone

Type a name on the **People** page (or on Home when the space is new) and press **Add**. **Add email or phone** lets you note contact details too. Both are optional.

### "This is me"

Tell ExpenseWaale which person is you, and Home speaks to you directly: "You owe ₹560" or "You get back ₹2,340". Set it with **This is me** on the People page, or from the "Which one is you?" prompt on Home. It's saved on this device for each space. If you track costs for a group you're not part of, choose **I'm not in this group**.

### Editing and removing

- Use the **pencil** icon to change a name, email or phone number.
- Use the **trash** icon to remove someone. Before anything happens, ExpenseWaale tells you exactly what changes: every expense that person paid is deleted too, and they're taken out of the other expenses they shared (those costs are then split between the people left). If they aren't part of any expense, nothing else changes.

## Adding and Editing Expenses

Click **Add expense** (or the **+** on a phone, or press **N**). Usually you only need two things:

1. **Amount**: what was paid, in rupees.
2. **What was it for?**: a short description like "Groceries" or "Electricity bill".

Everything else has a sensible default:

- **Paid by**: you, if you've said who you are, otherwise whoever paid last time.
- **Split between**: everyone in the space. Tap people to take them out, or tap **Everyone** to toggle all.
- **Category**: Misc. Tap another, or **New** to create your own.
- **Date**: today. Tap **Yesterday**, or **Other date** for a calendar.

As you fill it in, a line at the bottom explains the result, for example *"Aman, Kabir and Riya owe Dhruv ₹312.63 each."* Press **Add expense** (or Enter). A confirmation appears with **Undo** in case you got something wrong.

**To edit** an expense, click it anywhere it's listed (Home, Expenses or Balances). The same form opens with everything filled in. **Delete** is at the bottom left of the form, and asks you to confirm.

## How Splitting Works

- Every expense is split **equally** between the people you pick. ₹1,200 split between 4 people is ₹300 each.
- Each person's **share** is the total of their parts of the expenses they were in.
- Each person's **balance** is what they paid minus their share:
  - **gets back**: they paid more than their share
  - **owes**: they paid less than their share
  - **settled up**: they're even
- **Settle up** lists the fewest payments that bring everyone back to zero, for example "Kabir → Aman ₹1,800".

## Home: Where You Stand

Home covers the period that hasn't been settled yet: everything after the last period you marked as settled, or every expense if you've never settled.

- **The headline** says where you stand ("You owe ₹9,016.86", "You get back ₹2,340", "You're settled up"). If you haven't said who you are, it shows the total left to settle.
- **Settle up** lists the payments. From here you can:
  - **Copy summary**: copies a text version for your group chat.
  - **Share link**: creates a read-only page your group can open (see [Sharing](#sharing-with-your-group)).
  - **Mark as settled**: once everyone has paid, records the period as settled. Nothing is deleted. The next settle-up starts the day after, and you can undo it straight away.
  - **Details**: opens Balances for the same period.
- **Balances** shows everyone's position, with what they paid and their share.
- **Recent expenses** shows the latest five. Click one to edit it.

## Balances

The **Balances** page works out balances and the settle-up plan for any period you choose.

### Choosing a period

Click the period button for quick choices: **Since last settle-up**, **This month**, **Last month**, **All time**, and every period you've marked as settled. You can also pick a start and end date on the calendar. Your choice is remembered (it's shared with Insights).

### Filters

**Filters** opens a panel above the results:

- **Paid by**: only expenses these people paid for
- **Includes any of**: at least one of them is in the split
- **Includes all of**: every one of them is in the split
- **Leave out**: hide anything these people paid for or are in
- **Only these categories** and **Leave out** categories

Active filters appear as chips you can remove one by one, or all at once with **Clear all**. While filters are on, the page reminds you that the balances only count matching expenses.

### What's on the page

- A summary of total spent, number of expenses, and the amount left to settle
- **Settle up**, with **Share link** and **Mark period as settled**. Filters aren't saved with a settled period: the whole date range is marked.
- **Balances** for each person
- **Expenses** in the period, with sorting (newest, oldest, amount, title)
- **Settled periods**: click one to view it, or use the trash icon to remove it (that period counts as unsettled again; no expenses are deleted)

### Exports

**Export** offers:

- **Settlement report (PDF)**: balances, payments, and each person's expenses
- **Expense list (PDF)**: every expense in the period
- **Settle-up summary** and **Expenses by who paid**, copied as text

Exports use the period and filters currently on screen.

## Insights

Three tabs at the top of the page:

### Spending

- Total spent, average per day, average per person, and the biggest category
- **Spending over time** as daily, weekly or monthly bars. The table icon switches to the same numbers as a table.
- **By category**: what each category cost and its share of the total
- **Biggest days**: the five most expensive days
- Uses the same period and filters as Balances. **Download PDF** exports a summary.

### Per person

Each person's share of the costs (what they consumed, whoever paid), for all time, a custom range, or any settled period. You can also limit it to some categories. **Download PDF** exports the table.

### Leaderboard

All-time bragging rights: the top three payers, badges (Most generous, Silent assassin, Big spender, Milk Bhai for groceries, Fuel King, Foodie), and full rankings by total paid. **Download PDF** exports it.

## Sharing with Your Group

**Share link** (on Home or Balances) creates a read-only page and copies its address. Anyone with the link can open it. Nobody needs an account.

On the shared page, people can:

- **Find their share**: pick their name to see what they owe or get back, who to pay, and every expense they were part of
- See **who pays whom**, everyone's balances, and the full expense list

A shared link is a **snapshot**. Expenses added or edited later don't change it, so share a new link after updates.

## Tips and Troubleshooting

**Home says I owe nothing, but I know I do.**
Check who you've marked as "me". Home also only counts expenses after your last settled period. Open **Details** or **Balances → All time** to see everything.

**Balances shows no expenses.**
The period or filters may exclude them. Use **Show all time**, pick another period, or **Clear all** filters. The period you pick is shared across spaces, so after switching space you may need to pick it again.

**I can't delete a space.**
The Default space can't be deleted, and other spaces must have no people and no expenses first.

**Someone left the group.**
Removing a person also deletes the expenses they paid. If you want to keep history, settle up and mark the period settled first, and consider leaving them in.

**Uneven splits?**
Splits are always equal. For something only some people share, pick just those people in **Split between**. For different amounts, add separate expenses.

## Quick Reference

- **Add an expense**: Add expense button, the **+** on a phone, or press **N**
- **Edit or delete an expense**: click it
- **See who owes whom**: Home, or Balances for any period
- **Settle up**: follow the payments, then **Mark as settled**
- **Share with the group**: **Share link**, **Copy summary**, or a PDF from **Export**
- **Switch group**: the space switcher

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Space | A separate group with its own people, expenses and settled periods |
| Share | A person's part of the costs they were in |
| Balance | What someone paid minus their share |
| Settle up | The fewest payments that bring everyone to zero |
| Settled period | A date range you've marked as paid up; the next settle-up starts after it |
| Share link | A read-only snapshot your group can open without an account |
